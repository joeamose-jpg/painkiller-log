Lower my limits

Only lower these if a pharmacist or doctor told you to, for example because you weigh under 50 kg or have a liver or kidney condition. You cannot raise them above the standard adult limit.

Check before you take
Paracetamol
  • Ask a pharmacist first if you weigh under 50 kg, have liver or kidney problems, or drink heavily or binge drink. Your limit may be lower.
  • Do not take it with other medicines that contain paracetamol, such as cold and flu remedies and co-codamol.
  • Check first if you take metoclopramide, domperidone, warfarin or flucloxacillin.
Ibuprofen
  • Not for you, or ask first, if you have had an allergic reaction to ibuprofen, aspirin or another anti-inflammatory, a stomach ulcer, asthma, a heart, liver or kidney condition, blood clotting problems or a stroke.
  • Avoid it if you are pregnant or trying to get pregnant unless a doctor or pharmacist says it is fine.
  • Ask first if you take blood thinners such as warfarin, steroids, antidepressants or blood pressure tablets.
  • Do not take it with another anti-inflammatory such as aspirin or naproxen.
  • Take it with or after food. Large amounts of alcohol make side effects more likely.
Co-codamol
  • It contains paracetamol, so it counts towards your paracetamol total.
  • It contains codeine, which can cause drowsiness and addiction. Over the counter it is for short-term use only.

Ibuprofen and paracetamol can be taken together if needed. The NHS suggests trying each on its own first, using the lowest dose that works, and stopping when the pain has gone.

About this page and sources

What it does. It adds up what you log over any rolling 24 hours and compares it with adult limits for each medicine and each active ingredient. A medicine with the same ingredient as another, such as co-codamol and paracetamol, shares one total.

What it does not do. The interaction checks are a short built-in list, not a full drug database. It does not know your weight, health or other medicines. The leaflet in your box, a pharmacist or a doctor always comes first. Limits were checked against the sources below on 13 November 2026.

Where your log is kept. On this device, and in your account when you are signed in. Doses older than 7 days are removed.
